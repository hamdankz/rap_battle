🎙️ RECORD YOUR VOICE

Your voice becomes a secret MC in the game. Each player gets a random household voice and finds out whose it was at the end.

What to say (about 1–2 minutes)

1. Normal voice:

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Nobody expected the bus to arrive early, but there it was, right on time. Check, one, two, three, four.

2. Hype voice, like an MC:

Yo, step up, you're not ready! I'm the king of this house and I run this place!

3. In rhythm, tapping a beat:

I woke up early, made some tea, nobody's better than me, you'll see.

Quiet room, phone 10–15 cm from your mouth, steady volume. Repeat the three parts if you need to fill a minute.
