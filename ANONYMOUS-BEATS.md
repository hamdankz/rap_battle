# Anonymous per-player beats

Each human player chooses one of three face-down beat cards while writing four lines. The server privately assigns three different randomly shuffled styles out of 15 to that player each round. The card labels A/B/C do not reveal the styles. The choice is submitted together with lyrics; no player can see another player’s choice beforehand. At performance time, the chosen style is revealed on the battle screen and the host switches beats for each MC. Each performance has its own one-measure intro and an instrumental that loops until the voice ends. The syllable estimator uses groups of 2, 3, or 5 measures. AI challengers choose randomly.

The 15 styles are original procedural drum/bass patterns, not licensed sample tracks. The styles are intentionally lightweight; some are variations on a shared synth engine. The game and custom /api/voice endpoint are preserved.

Test in two browsers (host and phone) before inviting guests. Kokoro still requires a browser download and has not been tested live in this environment.
